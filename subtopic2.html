Harnessing Social Media to Drive Sales TaylorAnn Washburn and Mallory Rahe Social media has become a powerful tool for businesses looking to increase sales, attract customers, and build stronger relationships with their audience. This article explains how businesses can use platforms such as Facebook and Instagram to promote their products, share engaging content, and reach potential customers. It highlights the importance of setting clear goals, understanding a target audience, and creating posts that capture people's attention. The article also discusses how businesses can track their social media performance to see what strategies are working and where improvements can be made. Rather than simply posting pictures or updates, businesses can use social media as part of a larger marketing strategy. This source is valuable because it demonstrates how effective social media planning can help businesses grow, increase customer engagement, and create lasting connections with their audience.

Social Media Marketing for Small Businesses: A Practical Guide Sara Siems, Hyejune Park, Courtney Bir, and Audrey King For small businesses, social media offers an exciting opportunity to reach new customers, promote products, and build a stronger online presence. This article discusses how businesses can choose the right social media platforms based on their goals and the audiences they want to attract. It highlights the importance of creating engaging content, including photos, videos, stories, and helpful information that encourages people to interact with a business. The article also explains how businesses can use social media analytics to measure engagement, track performance, and understand which marketing strategies are most effective. By paying attention to these results, businesses can make improvements and strengthen their online marketing efforts. This source is valuable because it provides practical strategies that small businesses can use to connect with customers, increase brand awareness, and support long-term growth.
