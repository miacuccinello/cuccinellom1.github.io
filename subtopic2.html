Harnessing Social Media to Drive Sales TaylorAnn Washburn and Mallory Rahe This article explains how businesses can use social media to increase sales and connect with customers. It discusses the importance of setting clear goals, planning social media posts, creating interesting content, and checking how well posts perform. Businesses can use platforms such as Facebook and Instagram to promote products, attract potential customers, and encourage people to make purchases. The article also explains why businesses should understand their target audience and create content that interests them. This source is helpful because it shows that social media is more than just posting pictures or updates. With a good plan, businesses can use it to build customer relationships and support growth.

Social Media Marketing for Small Businesses: A Practical Guide Sara Siems, Hyejune Park, Courtney Bir, and Audrey King This article explains different ways small businesses can use social media to reach customers and promote their products or services. It discusses choosing the right social media platforms, creating interesting posts, growing an audience, and measuring results. Businesses should choose platforms based on their goals and the types of customers they want to reach. The article also explains that sharing photos, videos, stories, and helpful information can make content more interesting. Businesses can use analytics to see how many people interact with their posts and whether their marketing efforts are working. This source is useful because it gives practical advice that small businesses can follow to improve their social media marketing.
