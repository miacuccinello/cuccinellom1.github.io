Harnessing Social Media to Drive Sales TaylorAnn Washburn and Mallory Rahe Social media has become a valuable resource for businesses looking to increase sales, attract customers, and build stronger relationships with their audience. This article clarifies how businesses can use platforms, such as Facebook and Instagram, to promote their products, share engaging content, as well as reach potential customers. It explains the value of setting goals, understanding a target audience, and developing posts that capture people's attention. The article also discusses how businesses can follow their social media performance to see what strategies are running smoothly and where improvements can be made. Rather than simply posting pictures or updates, businesses can use social media as part of a larger marketing strategy. This source is important because it demonstrates how impactful social media planning can help businesses grow, increase customer engagement, and create long-term connections with their audience.

Social Media Marketing for Small Businesses: A Practical Guide Sara Siems, Hyejune Park, Courtney Bir, and Audrey King For small businesses, social media provides an exciting opportunity to reach new customers, promote products, and build a stronger online presence. This article explains how businesses select social media platforms based on their goals and the audiences they want to reach. It emphasizes the importance of creating engaging content... including photos, videos, and supportive information that encourages people to interact with a business. The article also talks about how businesses can use social media analytics to measure engagement, track performance, and identify which marketing strategies are most powerful. By using this information, businesses can make improvements and strengthen their online marketing presence. This source is valuable because it provides realistic strategies that small businesses can use to connect with customers, increase brand awareness, and support long-term growth.
