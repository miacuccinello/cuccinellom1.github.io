Stay Safe Online: Kids and Teens Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) This article explains the dangers that children and teenagers may face when using social media and other online platforms. It discusses online predators, cyberbullying, privacy concerns, and the importance of protecting personal information. The FBI explains that people should be careful about what they post because information shared online may not remain private. The article also encourages users to avoid communicating with strangers and to recognize suspicious messages or requests. It provides advice about keeping accounts private, protecting passwords, and reporting dangerous online behavior. This source is helpful because it teaches young people how to recognize online threats and make safer decisions when using social media.

Social Media and Youth Mental Health U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office of the Surgeon General This article discusses how social media can affect the mental health and well-being of children and teenagers. It explains that social media can help people connect with others, but it may also create risks. Spending too much time on social media can be associated with anxiety, depression, and other mental health concerns. The article also discusses how comparing yourself to others online may negatively affect body image and self-esteem. It encourages young people and families to develop healthy social media habits and create boundaries around screen time. This source is useful because it explains the possible mental health risks of social media and offers suggestions for using these platforms more responsibly.
