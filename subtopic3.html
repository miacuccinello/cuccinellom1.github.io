Stay Safe Online: Kids and Teens Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) While social media has become a popular way to communicate and share information, it is essential to comprehend the potential dangers that come with using these platforms. This article from the FBI discusses numerous online risks that children and teenagers may face... including cyberbullying, online predators, scams, as well as privacy concerns. It emphasizes the importance of protecting personal information and thinking very carefully before posting, since anything shared online may not always be private. The article also encourages young users to understand suspicious behavior, avoid interacting with strangers, and report anything at all that makes them feel uncomfortable/unsafe. Additionally, it provides helpful suggestions for creating strong passwords and keeping online accounts secure. This source is extremely valuable because it raises awareness about the risks of social media and helps young people learn how to protect themselves while navigating the online world.

Social Media and Youth Mental Health U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office of the Surgeon General The influence of social media goes beyond online communication, as it can also influence the mental health as well as well-being of children and teenagers. This article from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services explores both the benefits and risks of using social media. While these platforms allow for young people to interact with another, overuse may be associated with anxiety, depression, and other mental health concerns. The article also highlights how continuously comparing yourself to others online can negatively affect self-esteem and body image. It emphasizes the significance of developing healthy social media habits, setting boundaries with screen time, and finding a balance between online and real-life activities. This source is important because it raises awareness about the possible mental health effects of social media and encourages the youth to use these platforms in more responsible ways.
